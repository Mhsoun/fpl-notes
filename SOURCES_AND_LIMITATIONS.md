## Evidence labels

- **USER-REPORTED:** the user explicitly reports an action or outcome. Reliable as a record of what the user said; not automatically an independent account verification.
- **USER-INTENT:** the user requests, prefers, proposes or considers a choice. It may later be revised.
- **ASSISTANT-PROPOSAL:** an earlier assistant recommendation or projected lineup. Not a completed transaction.
- **ASSISTANT-CLAIM:** an earlier assistant asserted completion, scores or other facts without independently recovered supporting evidence.
- **ARCHIVE-ONLY:** a detail is in the previous archive or retained summary, but its underlying message was not recovered in this import.
- **UNKNOWN:** not established by available evidence.

## Important corrections and unresolved issues

### Isak/Janelt in GW7

A 16 October assistant recommendation put Isak first on the bench. The recovered 18 October user wording is: **'Guess what! Did I not say isak start ?'** A nearby assistant reply reported Isak 9 points and Janelt -1 and discussed 'Isak benching'. This does not independently establish the submitted fantasy XI: a real-life start and a fantasy start are different facts. A retrieval summary also conflicted about who was benched. Therefore the repository records the bench recommendation, the user's challenge and assistant-reported scores, but does **not** assert a verified 10-point loss or a verified submitted bench order.

### Transfer and chip completion

An assistant said Muharemović → De Cuyper was completed and Haaland Triple Captain was used. Direct user confirmation was not recovered. The user did explicitly report using Bench Boost in the 25 October discussion. Wildcard intent is clear; activation and the final submitted 15 are not independently established.

### Latest squad versus older drafts

The 30 October afternoon wildcard draft included Van Dijk, Maatsen and Kudus and was previously valued at about £101.4m. Later recommendations instead included Van Hecke, De Cuyper and Groß. Preserve those as separate snapshots; do not infer the transactions that connected them. The 5 November recommendation is the latest recovered recommendation, not a live team read.

### Other limits

Dates are discussion dates; gameweek labels follow those discussions and have not been reconciled against official deadlines. Historical club labels, FPL eligibility, positions, fixtures, prices, injuries, points and expected-points figures may be wrong or stale. No new sports claims are being made by preserving them. Exact early-week squads, complete scores, rival standings and screenshots are missing. Do not fill those gaps by guessing.
