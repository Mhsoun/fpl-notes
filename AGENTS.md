# Working with this football-project archive

## Scope

This repository records the owner's Fantasy Premier League 2026/27 discussions and the Venditos mini-league project. It is not the separate football prediction software project. Keep unrelated personal, family, employment, medical, financial and business information out of it. Public publication of these football notes was authorized on 6 November 2026; do not add credentials, session cookies or unreviewed full-account exports.

## Read first

1. `SOURCES_AND_LIMITATIONS.md` for evidence labels and known contradictions.
2. `strategy/preferences.md` for the owner's constraints.
3. `squads/latest-known.md` and `squads/snapshots.md` for dated recommendations, not assumed current holdings.
4. The relevant gameweek file and `DECISION_LOG.md` before changing an agreed plan.

## Evidence rules

- Distinguish USER-REPORTED, USER-INTENT, ASSISTANT-PROPOSAL, ASSISTANT-CLAIM, ARCHIVE-ONLY and UNKNOWN.
- A recommendation, screenshot placeholder, question such as 'lock it?', or an earlier assistant saying 'confirmed' is not proof of a completed FPL transaction.
- Do not convert historical player prices, club labels, injuries, fixtures, scores or expected-points estimates into current facts.
- Do not infer complete squads from partial lists. Do not assign missing early-season transfers to invented gameweeks.
- When sources conflict, retain the contradiction and prefer direct user evidence over an assistant's interpretation. Distinguish a real-life start from a fantasy starting-XI selection.
- Preserve old snapshots; append a dated correction or new snapshot rather than silently rewriting history.

## Future analysis

Verify the actual squad, bank, selling prices, free transfers, chips and official deadline before new recommendations. Research current injuries, likely minutes and fixtures when giving current advice, and record source dates. Do not use the old £101.4m wildcard draft as the current team.

The standing preferences include Liverpool representation, a preference for 4-3-3 rather than five defenders, and avoiding Harry Maguire. Recorded 3-4-3 recommendations do not silently repeal those preferences. Explicitly explain any proposed exception and any change to an agreed plan.

Copy `templates/gameweek.md` for new weeks. Record transfers and captaincy as proposed until the user or reliable account data confirms submission. Record outcomes separately from projections. No GitHub sync or new automation is configured by this import.
