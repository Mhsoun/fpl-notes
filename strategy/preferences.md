# Preferences, objectives and decision style

Sources: S01–S05 and S09. These are the owner's football-project preferences, not independently verified competition facts.

## Objectives

- Fantasy Premier League **2026/27**; mini-league **Venditos**.
- Maximize expected points and compete for first place; examine the leaderboard and top three rivals when current data is available.
- When asked to focus on a specific week, prioritize that week. The user explicitly rejected expanding GW8 into a GW8–12 plan without reason.
- No strategy guarantees maximum points. Record the upside, uncertainty and trade-offs instead of promising a guaranteed result.

## Squad preferences

- Liverpool supporter; repeatedly requested **at least two Liverpool players**.
- Prefer **4-3-3** and dislike five-defender formations. Later 3-4-3 proposals are historical recommendations, not a silently changed standing preference.
- **Do not recommend Harry Maguire.**
- Strongly values Isak's consistent performances and challenged selling or benching him without a clear reason.
- Challenged removing Gabriel, Van Dijk and Groß; wants the replacement's expected benefit explained rather than change for its own sake.
- A wildcard permits rebuilding from scratch, but does not require removing every useful existing player.

## Research and communication

Use current news and evidence for actual future advice, not repeated paraphrases of the user's assumptions. Compare realistic alternatives by expected minutes, role, fixture, risk, budget and likely points. Explain why an established plan changes, especially close to a deadline.

For captaincy, compare ceiling and playing-time certainty; also name a viable vice-captain. Give an explicit starting XI and ordered bench, not just a transfer shortlist.

## Timing and budget

The user has sometimes preferred waiting until Friday or the day before deadline for injuries and remaining matches, but later asked to act immediately when waiting appeared pointless. Evaluate the benefit of information against price risk; neither 'always wait' nor 'always buy early' is a standing rule.

Historical amounts such as £0.2m bank, an estimated £0.5m price impact and £1.0m spare belong to different squad snapshots. Never merge them into a current balance. Check official selling values and bank before calling a draft affordable.

## Position and concentration concerns

The user explicitly pointed out that a midfielder cannot be directly replaced with a defender. Also questioned concentration in a single club and asked whether diversification would improve opportunities. Preserve these as constraints/questions, not a rule to sacrifice good selections solely for variety.
